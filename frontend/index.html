Tips for Best Results
Good Lighting

Take photos in natural daylight. Avoid shadows falling on the leaf surface.

Focus on Symptoms

Capture the area with the most visible spots, lesions, or discoloration clearly.

Fill the Frame

Hold the leaf close so it fills most of the photo for accurate classification.

No Blur

Keep the camera steady. A blurry image reduces the accuracy of the AI diagnosis.
